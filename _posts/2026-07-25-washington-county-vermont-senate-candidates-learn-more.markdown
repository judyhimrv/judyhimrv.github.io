### Did you know there are 4 people running for 3 Vermont Senate seats in Washington County (that's us!)?  

Three incumbents (Anne Watson, Ann Cummings and Andrew Perchlik) and a newcomer, Gabe Lajeunesse. If you want to know more about them before the August primary, here's one way: 

### Come to a forum on Wednesday, July 29th at 5:30 pm at Montpelier City Hall.  

It will also be live streamed on [The Bridge's website](https://thebridgevt.org/?emci=addf043c-7d80-f111-b337-000d3a1558ce&emdi=531907ba-2784-f111-b337-000d3a1558ce&ceid=2500793) and [ORCA media](https://www.orcamedia.net/series/live-stream-events?emci=addf043c-7d80-f111-b337-000d3a1558ce&emdi=531907ba-2784-f111-b337-000d3a1558ce&ceid=2500793).
