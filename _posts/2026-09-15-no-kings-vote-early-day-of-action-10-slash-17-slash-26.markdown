### NK4!!! It's Get Out The Vote (GOTV) in the MRV!
Not one, not two, not three but FOUR locations/events in the valley!

Saturday, October 17, NK4 is only about getting out the vote - 
EARLY - to make sure everyone votes.  When we vote, we win.

The NK4 coalition, which includes Indivisible National, is not a protest. They've hosted that three times. We've all been there! NK4 is about PROTECTING the VOTE.
